Sensor project / Gentofte Kommune

Usage insight strengthens accessibility in Gentofte

When capacity is limited, data becomes the key to using spaces better. In Gentofte, the focus is accessible and charging spaces.

Challenge

What needed to be understood.

Gentofte wanted better conditions for people with disabilities while also understanding how selected charging spaces are used.

Approach

From measurement to clarity.

Papp installed sensors on selected accessible and charging spaces to follow occupancy and demand more closely.

Outcome

A stronger basis for decisions.

The insight gives Gentofte a better basis for practical decisions such as signage, time limits and distribution of existing spaces.
