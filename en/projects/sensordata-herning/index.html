Sensor project / Herning Kommune

Sensor data opens new parking opportunities in Herning

Two parking areas. Same address. Very different occupancy. In Herning, data revealed hidden resources and opened a path toward smarter parking decisions.

Challenge

What needed to be understood.

In central Herning, a street-level parking area and an adjacent parking basement showed noticeably different occupancy. The task was to understand where capacity was being overlooked and why.

Approach

From measurement to clarity.

Papp used sensor data to compare occupancy, duration and behaviour across the two areas and turn the difference into questions the municipality could act on.

Outcome

A stronger basis for decisions.

The project gives Herning a stronger basis for thinking in solutions that use the existing parking capacity better before expanding.
